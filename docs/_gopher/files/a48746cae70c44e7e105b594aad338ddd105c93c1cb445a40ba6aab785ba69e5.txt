EMCICalc brings exact mathematics, a scientific keypad, graphs, data tools and reusable workspaces to Android. Start with a normal calculation, then use **MENU** to open a tool without clearing the calculator.

The illustrations show the actual Android interface rendered on a Linux host. They explain the controls; they are not photographs or captures from an Android device. System bars, keyboard, file picker and physical screen size can differ on your device.

## Start here

New to EMCICalc? [Install and open the application](01-install.md), then [make your first calculation](02-first-calculation.md). Already using it? Choose a chapter below, or use **Search the manual** to find a control or worksheet.

This manual uses English interface labels and covers **version 1.0.0**. [Save and export your work](13-save-restore.md) before replacing a device or removing the application.
