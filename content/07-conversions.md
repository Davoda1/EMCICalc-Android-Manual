On Compact, **SHIFT+1** opens Constants and **SHIFT+2** opens Unit converter. The Toolbox also provides conversion tools. Search the catalogs and select an entry to insert the recognized mathematical name or unit.

## Convert a temperature

1. Open **Unit converter**.
2. Enter `25` in **Value**.
3. Choose **Degree Celsius** as **From unit** and **Degree Fahrenheit** as **To unit**.
4. Tap **Convert**. The result is `77 °F`.
5. Use **Copy result** or **Insert result** as needed.

Compound units and prefixes are supported when their dimensions are compatible. A metre cannot be converted to a second. Temperature conversion includes offsets, not just multiplication. **Swap** exchanges source and destination; press Convert to calculate the new direction.

The converter has its own saved Exact/Decimal presentation. Its S⇔D control reformats the stored conversion. Inspecting or inserting a conversion does not automatically commit a main-calculator History entry.

## Currency rates

Open **Toolbox → Currency converter**, enter **Amount**, choose **From currency** and **To currency**, then convert. The tool uses dated **European Central Bank reference rates**. It refreshes when needed and provides a manual refresh control.

A first successful download is required before cached rates can be used offline. Check the displayed date, source and stale/failure status. Only currencies available in the supported rate table can be selected. Reference conversion does not include a bank's spread or fees; Finance has a separate conversion-after-fees worksheet. Calculation amounts and expressions are not sent with the public-rate download.
